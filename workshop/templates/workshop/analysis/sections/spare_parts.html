{% load custom_filters %} {% comment %} THE SHOP ROUTE, ON ITS OWN SECTION. A part bought from a spare shop for one car: it has a shop, an ordering state, and a payable behind it. Its twin section, Inventory, has none of those — a draw comes off a shelf already paid for by a restock bill. They were one section with two tables until 2026-08-25, and before that one merged table. The split finished here: the HEADLINE was still combined, so a per-job trading margin was being averaged against a shelf margin that depends on `avg_cost` being right, and the blended figure described nothing. ⚠ NO QUANTITY COLUMN, deliberately. A shop row's quantity moves no money — the cost IS the line total as typed off the shop's own bill — so a quantity beside a cost invites dividing one by the other and reading a rate the workshop was never billed. Inventory has the column, because there a quantity is what actually left the shelf. ⚠ "MARGIN", NOT "PROFIT". Parts sold less parts cost, with no labour in it and no overhead off it. `Profit` is the Profit page's word; `Gross profit` is revenue less parts cost (car profiles, mechanics); this is neither. {% endcomment %}
