"Spend" is what the work cost — the figure the Profit page charges. "Paid" is cash that actually left the drawer against these shops' ledgers, on their own instalment rhythm. Neither affects the other and neither belongs inside the profit equation: an owner who subtracts a cash figure from a profit figure gets a number that is not anything.
