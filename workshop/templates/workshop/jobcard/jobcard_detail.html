Rebuilt 2026-08-18 on the owner's report that it was "not visually comfortable", and on their own suggestion of what to copy. The owner offered a second option worth recording, because it is the one that was NOT taken: delete this page and point everything that reaches it at the edit form instead, on the reasoning that a settled card is locked anyway so it is safe to scroll. The lock is real but it is not the common case — it covers PAID and BULK_PAID only, and the cards people open most are the ones still on the floor, which open fully editable. Sending a read to an editable form means every glance is one stray tap from a change, on a tablet, on the longest form in the app; and this page is `@staff_required`, so Floor reads cards here. Nine places link or redirect here. A read-only view is a different job from an edit, and the answer was to make it look like it belongs rather than to remove it. WHAT IT COPIES, AND FROM WHERE. Two vocabularies already existed and both are reused verbatim rather than re-invented: · The SECTION BAND is the job-card form's `.jc-sec-head`, value for value — the same `#f8fafc` band, the same 34px slate icon badge, the same title size and weight. This page is that form's read-only twin, and the two are opened minutes apart; they were drifting into looking like different products, which is the whole complaint. Read those values off the FORM if you ever change them, not off CLAUDE.md, which still describes an older filled-blue band the form no longer has. · The LISTS are the dashboard car card's live-details drawer — a row is an icon and a line, with a quiet subtext under it. That is the owner's named reference and it replaces two horizontally-scrolling TABLES. A table on a read-only page is the worst of both: six columns of ordering workflow that have to be scrolled sideways to read, on a page whose only job is to be read. The drawer shape needs no scroller at any width. Nothing here is editable and nothing posts. The only controls are Invoice and Edit in the header. {% endcomment %} {% block extra_css %} {% endblock %} {% block content %} {% comment %} The two rail exceptions are tested exactly as `jobcard_form.html` tests them — against `car_color` itself, never against the resolved hex — so the two headers can never disagree about one car.
