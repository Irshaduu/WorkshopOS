It briefly was an editable field, to cover a raise entered before the previous month was settled. That solved by interface what the workshop's own order of work already solves: settle the finished month first, then apply the hike. The salary is frozen onto the line at that moment, so the settled month keeps what was actually earned and later months use the new figure. If the order does go wrong, the remedy is to delete the settlement and settle again — an Owner action, deliberate and logged.
