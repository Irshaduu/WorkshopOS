What was understood is spelled out underneath ("Fri 10 Apr 2026"). * THE TOTAL IS WORKED OUT, NEVER TYPED — labour plus every part amount, the figure the server saves — and sits on the right with one line under it asking for it to be checked against the XL bill. A wrong figure is fixed in its own line. (It was typed as a check until the owners chose the eye, 2026-09-17.) * ENTER NEVER SAVES. It moves to the next box, so half a bill cannot be saved by a reflex. * SAVE & ADD NEXT keeps the month and year, and starts again on Day. * ONE OPEN ROW for jobs and for parts. Typing into it opens the next, so there is always exactly one waiting and never a screen of empty boxes to scroll past. * THE JOB CARD'S OWN HABITS: the plate turns to capitals as it is typed, a known plate fills MAKE and MODEL when the box is left, and MAKE and MODEL suggest through the same dropdown (`script.js`). There is NO customer name box — not needed on these bills (the owners' call, 2026-09-17). * DELETE WAITS IN THE ⋮ AT THE TOP of an edit, never beside the Save buttons where a hand reaching for Save could land on it, and outside the bill's form. * JOB PERFORMED and PART NAME suggest through the Job Card's own dropdown — a list under the box, never a , which a phone or tablet shows as a strip above the keyboard instead. JOB PERFORMED offers the Job Card's lines (a part + "replaced" …); PART NAME offers this bill's job lines with the verb taken off, then the warehouse CATEGORY names (a bill prints "Engine Oil", never the brand), then the Spare Parts master list. Read only: nothing typed here is added to either list. * FILL FROM PDF (Add page only) posts the bill's own PDF to `old_bill_from_pdf`, which draws this form filled (`workshop/old_bill_pdf.py`) and saves nothing; the PDF's printed TOTAL rides in `pdf_total` and is compared under the TOTAL. Every rule is decided in `workshop/old_bills.py`; the browser copy in `old-bill-core.js` only says what the server will say, a round trip sooner. ================================================================================ {% endcomment %} {% block extra_css %} {% endblock %} {% block content %}
