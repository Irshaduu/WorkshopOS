TWO AUDIENCES ON ONE PAGE, and the split is resolved in the view (`can_manage` / `can_see_prices`), never inferred from groups here. Office / Owner — everything: the price column, the price box, Edit, Delete. Floor — add only. No price is shown and no price box is rendered, because Floor is shown cost nowhere else in this app; and the row they add stores a NULL price, which Office fills in when the shop's bill is keyed. Hiding a control here is presentation and nothing more. The real halves are the decorators on `unassigned_spare_edit` and `spare_shop_delete_unassigned`, and the `PRICE_NOT_SUPPLIED` strip in `unassigned_spare_add` — a hidden input is one crafted POST away from writing a price, which is AUD-0081 all over again. TWO LAYOUT RULES WORTH NOT REDISCOVERING. (a) The add form is ONE horizontal scroller, not a grid that stacks, and since 2026-08-17 it scrolls at EVERY width including the laptop. Stacked it is eight full-width rows — the Save button lands well below the fold and the whole form has to be scrolled past to reach the list underneath it. Scrolling sideways is also the gesture staff already use on the Job Card's Spare Parts table, which is the screen this one sits beside in their day. It used to wrap above 768px instead, which gave one row of boxes two different shapes depending on the screen it was opened on. (b) The row actions are two inline BUTTONS, not a ⋮ dropdown. A dropdown inside a horizontal scroller is clipped — `overflow-x: auto` computes `overflow-y` to `auto` as well, and Popper cannot escape a clipping ancestor, which is the trap `.cb-list` already cost an afternoon for. Two buttons also take one tap instead of two on the tablet. Delete still opens a confirmation, so the destructive action is no closer than it was. {% endcomment %} {% block extra_css %} {% endblock %} {% block content %} {% if can_manage %}
