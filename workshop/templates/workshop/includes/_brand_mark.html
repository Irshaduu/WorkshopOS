{% comment %} The Formula D lockup — wordmark, rule and tagline — as the owner's own artwork, inlined as a data URI. WHY THE ARTWORK AND NOT A TRACE. Two auto-traced SVGs were tried. The first was 98% anti-aliasing noise (letterforms shredded into grey bands) and was rejected outright; the second was a clean two-colour vector and was actually shipped for half a day — but a trace is an approximation of letterforms by construction, and the owner could see the difference against the real mark. This file *is* the mark, so "looks exactly like the bill" stops being a matter of how good the trace was. WHY A DATA URI AND NOT ``. Same reason the wordmark was set in text before there was a logo file: anything fetched can fail to arrive, and a bill that prints without its letterhead is worse than one that never had it. A data URI is part of the document — no request, no static-file dependency, nothing to 404 after a deploy, and nothing for a CSP to permit. It also keeps the printed pages' promise of loading nothing from anywhere. WHY A RASTER IS SAFE HERE. The old note warned that "a raster one prints soft", and it was right about the rasters it meant. This one is 1323px across 56mm — 600 DPI, twice what a 300 DPI print consumes. It is cropped to its ink (the supplied canvas carried whitespace that would have shrunk the printed logo) and its 253-grey background is lifted to pure white so no box shows on the sheet. ONE copy, included by the invoice and the estimate — those go to one customer days apart and their letterhead must not be able to drift. Regenerate with `scratchpad/build_logo_png.py` if the artwork changes. Do not hand-edit the data below. {% endcomment %}
